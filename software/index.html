How we work

Four steps, and you see something you can click before the invoice gets large.

Scoping call

We walk through the process as it runs today, including the spreadsheet workarounds and the parts nobody likes admitting to. Most of the real requirements are hiding in those.

Data model and a fixed quote

We design the schema first, write down what the system will and will not do, and quote a fixed price against that. The data model is what decides whether the thing still works in month four.

Something you can click, early

You get a working version with your own data in it well before the end, so corrections happen while they are still cheap. Feedback on a screen beats feedback on a document.

Live, then maintained

We deploy it, train whoever uses it daily, and stay on for changes. Software that nobody maintains quietly stops being used.

What we build it with

Web apps and business systems are built on Next.js and PostgreSQL. For mobile apps and games, we choose the right tools for your project on the scoping call.

Projects are quoted after a scoping call, since a dashboard and a full system are not the same job. The call costs nothing.

A system we built

Estate CRM, built for a firm selling land on installments

A land company was tracking hundreds of installment plans across spreadsheets and WhatsApp messages. Nobody could answer who owed money this month without an afternoon of adding up. We built the system that answers it in one screen, and it is now running with a live pilot client.

  • Estates, plots and allocations in one place
  • Customers with their own payment schedules
  • Receipts issued against each payment
  • Overdue installments flagged automatically
  • Full audit trail of who changed what
  • Works on the phone the sales team already carries
