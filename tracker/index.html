For real estate companies selling land and homes on installments

A free spreadsheet that shows who owes you money this month.

If working that out means opening three spreadsheets and phoning a colleague, this tracker saves you the afternoon. It is for companies selling plots or houses on installments. There is a land version and a homes version, and we send the one that matches what you sell. It is free to keep.

  • Every plot or house and its status: available, reserved, sold or fully paid.
  • Each buyer, what they bought, their schedule and what falls due next. The homes version also handles payments tied to construction stages.
  • Late installments turn red by themselves, and balances work themselves out.
  • One dashboard tab shows total collected, total outstanding and total overdue.
  • Works in Google Sheets, and downloads to Excel.
