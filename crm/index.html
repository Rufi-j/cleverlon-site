What it does

One system, from allocation to final payment.

Estates and plots

Every estate, every plot, and its real status: available, reserved, allocated or fully paid. No more guessing what is still for sale.

Customers and plans

Each customer, their plots, their installment schedule and what falls due next, on one screen instead of five.

Payments and receipts

Record a payment and the balance updates itself. A numbered receipt is generated, so what was paid is never in dispute.

Overdue flagged for you

Late installments surface on their own rather than waiting for someone to notice. You see who to chase before the month closes.

Audit trail

Who recorded what, and when. Every change is logged, which matters when a customer queries a balance from eight months ago.

Works on a phone

Your sales team is at the site, not at a desk. Allocations and payments can be recorded from where the work happens.
