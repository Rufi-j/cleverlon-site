Privacy policy

This page says what Cleverlon Concepts collects when you use cleverlon.com, why we collect it, where it goes and how to ask us to delete it.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Who we are

Cleverlon is a trading name of Cleverlon Concepts, a business registered in Nigeria. In this policy, “we” means Cleverlon Concepts.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this site, we collect what you type:

  • your name and your company name
  • your email address
  • your WhatsApp or phone number, if you give one
  • your answers to the form questions, such as what you sell, what you want built, your budget or your answers in the audit

We also record which page the form was sent from and when. If you arrived from a link with a source tag, we record that too.

If you book a call, Calendly collects your details to make the booking. If you message us on WhatsApp, we see your number and what you write.

Why we collect it

We use your details to reply to you, to send you the free tracker if you asked for it, and to send the occasional follow-up that is relevant to what you asked about. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Where it is stored

Form submissions go into Google Sheets, and our emails go through Gmail, both under our Google account. Bookings are stored in Calendly. WhatsApp messages stay on our phone.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your details. We use service providers to run the site and our tools: Netlify (hosting), Google (Sheets and Gmail), Calendly, Umami (analytics), Loom (video) and WhatsApp.

Analytics

We use Umami to count visits to the site. It records the pages visited, where visitors came from, their country, and their browser and device type. It does not use cookies. It also counts clicks on Book a call and on links to the audit, form submits and game starts, but it does not record what you type. The data is stored in the EU and kept for 6 months.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not set cookies, and we do not store anything in your browser with localStorage or sessionStorage. That includes the games and the spin wheel demo. The Loom video on the software page is loaded from Loom, which may set its own cookies when you play it. Calendly opens on its own site and works under its own policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for up to 24 months after our last contact. Then we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Write to hellocleverlon@gmail.com and we will deal with your request within 30 days. To stop follow-up emails, tell us and we will stop.

The law

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for visitors in the UK and EU, the principles of the GDPR.

Changes

If we change this page, we change the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this page: hellocleverlon@gmail.com.
